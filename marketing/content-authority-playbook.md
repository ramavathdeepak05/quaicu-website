## 1. The Core Framework

| Level | What the content contains | What the audience does | QUAICU translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Post** consistently | Ignores you | Another dev shop posting "we build apps" |
| 2 | + Their **Problems** | **Notices** you | "They know we're stuck on spreadsheets and can't scale" |
| 3 | + Useful **Solutions** | **Pays attention** | "They showed me which process to automate first" |
| 4 | + A new **Perspective** | Sees you as an **Authority** | "They build AI that's safe to put in front of customers, because they started in governance" |
| 5 | + **Don't Ask** for anything | **Waits** for your offer | "When we're ready to build, we'll call QUAICU" |

**Standard:** every post hits Level 3. One flagship piece a week (the blog post) hits Level 5.

## 7. The Post Formula

```
HOOK      → Name the problem in their words              (Level 2)
CONTEXT   → Why it happens / what it costs them
FIX       → 2–4 concrete, usable steps                   (Level 3)
TWIST     → The perspective others won't say             (Level 4)
CLOSE     → A takeaway or a question. Never a pitch.     (Level 5)
```

### Worked example (LinkedIn, founder voice, problem #2)

```
HOOK     "We want to use AI but don't know where to start."
         I hear this every week from business owners.
CONTEXT  Most start with the tool. They buy a subscription, try a chatbot,
         and six months later nothing in the business has changed.
FIX      1. List the tasks your team repeats every day.
         2. Circle the ones that follow clear rules.
         3. Pick the one that eats the most hours.
         4. Decide who checks the AI's work before it counts.
TWIST    Step 4 is the one everyone skips, and it's why most AI never
         leaves the pilot. Control isn't what slows AI down. It's what
         lets you trust it with real work.
CLOSE    Which task would you hand to AI first?
```

### Hook templates
- "If your business still runs on spreadsheets, you're paying for it in hours."
- "3 signs your software is holding back your growth."
- "Nobody tells you this about adding AI: the model is the easy part."
- "Stop buying AI tools. Build one AI capability that actually works."
- "Before you hire an agency to build your app, check these 4 things."
- "We started in AI governance. Here's what it taught us about building software."
- "Everyone says AI will replace your team. It mostly replaces the copy-paste."

### Close templates (no selling)
- "Which task would you automate first?"
- "Save this for your next planning meeting."
- "Send this to someone who's about to hire a developer."
- "The short version: build fast, stay in control."

## 18. Guardrails

- Every post useful on its own. One idea per post.
- **Never name clients** without written permission.

- Keep governance as the edge, not the headline: lead with the business problem, show the control as the reason to trust QUAICU.
- Voice check: would a founder say it across the table to a business owner?
