## 1. The idea

**Line:** Software service that doesn't fade.

**Campaign name:** *Month Twelve*. Most agencies are at their best in month one and slowly decay. We ask buyers to judge a vendor on month twelve instead, and show how QUAICU stays the same:
- **A forward deployed engineer (FDE) for every client.** One engineer who stays with your account, manages the relationship, works in your tools (remote by default, on site when needed) and signs off every delivery.
- **An AI agent** that works alongside the FDE and does the repeatable work the same way every time. It fixes minor bugs for a person to approve, and alerts the FDE the moment something major breaks.
- **Nobody waits on one person.** The FDE decides what counts as minor or major. If the FDE is away, a major issue goes to the next available FDE or relationship manager, or to the internal team, and out-of-hours approvals go to the on-call team.
- **A record of every action**, so nothing depends on anyone's memory, and anyone covering for your FDE can pick up where they left off.

**How to say it:** "forward deployed engineer" in full in posts for CTOs. In posts for UAE business owners, explain it once: "one engineer who works with your team, manages the work, and signs off everything our AI agent does".

**Why this story:** complaints about agencies were the largest group of buyer problems in the research (about 42 mentions): missed deadlines, vendors going silent, people swapped mid-project, status calls that hide slipped dates, code held hostage. Every post in this campaign answers one of them.

**Who it's for:**
- About 60% of posts: CTOs, heads of IT and operations at Indian mid-market firms, regulated sectors first, whose AI pilots aren't reaching real use.
- About 30%: owners of UAE small and lower mid-market businesses.
- About 10%: general posts that also work for the US, UK and Europe.

**The signature visual:** "The Slide". A chart of service quality over 12 months: a grey line that starts high and sags, and a QUAICU line that stays flat. It opens the campaign, returns in the year-end post and closes it. Labelled *Illustrative* every time, because it is a concept, not data.

**Posting rules for every post:**
- Upload the video natively (no YouTube links).
- No links in the post body. quaicu.org lives in the profile and the Featured section.
- First line under 210 characters, whole post under 1,300 characters, at most 3 hashtags.
- No em dashes, no hype words, no "excited to announce".
- Never name an agency or a client.
- Founders reply to every comment within the day, with real help.
- Comment with substance on 5 posts a day from target buyers (Indian CTOs and heads of IT, UAE business owners). This matters as much as posting.
