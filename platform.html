02·2 HOW THE ENGINE IS KEPT IN CHECK

Who may act. What the AI drafts.
Where people sign off.

PLANE · 01 Control
Who may act.

Defines who is allowed to act, under what authority, with whose approval. No AI action without defined authority.

PLANE · 02 Reasoning
AI drafts within limits.

The AI works inside the rules you set, producing drafts a person can review. The rules are the contract; the AI is the worker under them.

PLANE · 03 Execution
People sign off.

Critical rules cannot be bypassed and nothing ships without sign-off. The system never fails silently.
