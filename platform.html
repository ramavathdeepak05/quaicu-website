Authority, Intelligence, Enforcement.
Coordinated as one system.

PLANE · 01 Control Plane
Authority, Policy, Power.

Defines who is allowed to act, under what authority, with whose approval. No AI action without defined authority. No silent privilege escalation.

PLANE · 02 Reasoning Plane
Bounded Intelligence.

AI reasons inside declared rules and produces bounded, reviewable outcomes. Intelligence is permitted; improvisation is not.

PLANE · 03 Execution Plane
Enforcement & Safety.

Enforces absolute constraints, handles real-world uncertainty. Critical rules cannot be bypassed. The system never fails silently.
