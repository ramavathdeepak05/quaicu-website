## 6. ARCHITECTURE

**Headline:** Governed AI as institutional infrastructure.

**Sub-headline:** Not a product feature. Not a compliance bolt-on. The architecture itself.

---

### The Hybrid Governance Model

**Deterministic Core.** For finance, grades, payroll — zero-error tolerance. Rule-based execution, no AI override permitted.

**Probabilistic AI.** For predictions, risk flags, draft content — always human-reviewable, never autonomously executed.

The two operate side by side under the same governance kernel. Determinism handles what must not vary. AI handles what benefits from intelligence. Humans remain the final authority on consequential action.

---

### The Stack

**APPLICATION LAYER** — Vertical-specific modules and wizards across web and mobile. Today: 8 modules in ALIS. Tomorrow: vertical applications on the same kernel.

**RULE ENGINE** — Strict logic. Deterministic execution. No silent overrides.

**AI AGENTS** — Bounded intelligence. Probabilistic output. Always reviewable, never autonomous.

**TRUST LAYER** — On-premise, private cloud, or hybrid. Sovereign data storage. Identity management. Encryption at rest and in transit. Immutable audit ledger.

---

### Enforcement, Layer by Layer

Every state change in every QUAICU deployment passes through a non-bypassable enforcement model — HITL checkpoints, state machine validation, role-based access control, approval workflows, the policy engine, and global locks. The audit ledger is hash-chained and fully replayable.

Critical rules cannot be bypassed by an AI agent, an integrator, or a privileged user. The system never fails silently.
