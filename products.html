EPILOGUE

Why six?
Why one kernel?

Every vertical above shares the same regulatory pressure: explainability, auditability, tenant isolation, human override, traceable AI.

Building six separate platforms would mean building the same governance kernel six times and getting it wrong six times. QUAICU builds it once, inside Ring 0, and inherits it across every vertical application above.
