EPILOGUE

Why a reusable
foundation?

Every engagement needs the same things: rules checked before the AI acts, human approval, a record of every action and clean separation of client data.

Building these from scratch each time would mean rebuilding the same foundation again and again. QUAICU builds it once and starts every ticket and every build from it, so delivery is faster and more consistent.
