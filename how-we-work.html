10 HOW WE WORK

How we work.
Every step, in order.

Two ways to work with QUAICU: ongoing engineering support, priced per ticket, and new platform builds. Each starts with a conversation and is delivered by a dedicated relationship manager, our AI engine and a person reviewing every delivery.

  2. Scope
  3. Deliver
  4. Support ongoing

Talk

Scope

Deliver

Support

Five commitments QUAICU makes at every step.

Data isolation Per-tenant. Your data, your keys, your residency. No commingling, no exceptions.
Your work product You own what we deliver once it is paid for. QUAICU keeps its AI engine, Governance Kernel and tools.
Deployment posture On-premise, private cloud, or hybrid. Your IT policy decides.
No silent model swaps Every model change is a policy event in your ledger, with the rationale recorded. You see what changed, when, and why.
Your call at every step You can stop at any step. You decide what goes ahead and when.
10·3 WHAT VARIES

What we shape together, for you.

Every engagement is scoped with you. The first conversation shapes everything below.

01

Timeline

Depends on the size of the work. Builds are scoped against a timeline we agree with you, delivered 30% more efficiently; support follows your tickets.

02

Scope

What is included, and what is not. Agreed up front so everyone knows what to expect.

03

Who is involved

Who signs, who approves and who reviews on your side. We shape the conversation so each person sees what they need.

04

Cost shape

Support is priced per ticket: you pay for the tickets you raise. Builds are priced to the project, 30% more efficient in both price and timeline.

05

Approvals

Each step ends with your sign-off. You see what was delivered and what we recommend next.

06

Deployment and data

On-premise, private cloud or hybrid. Your data stays separate from every other client's, wherever it runs.
