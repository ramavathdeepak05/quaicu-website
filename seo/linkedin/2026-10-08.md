Guardrails are instructions inside the system they are meant to constrain.

Enforcement is a separate layer that decides what is allowed to run.

The difference matters when something goes wrong. A guardrail can be bypassed by a bug, a misconfiguration or a rushed admin. An enforced gate cannot.

AI proposes. Rules enforce. Humans approve.
