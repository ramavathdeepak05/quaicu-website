WHAT JEV IS

A model that decides,
not one that writes.

Jev reads text and answers structured questions about it in a single call. It returns probabilities rather than prose, so every decision can be thresholded, logged and checked.

Yes or no
The probability that something is true, for example “does this ticket need a person?”
Choice
One option from a list, with a probability for every option, for example which queue a ticket belongs to.
Score
A position on a scale of 2 to 10 levels, for example urgency or sentiment.
Price
$0.042 per million input tokens and no charge for output, on TypeSafe's published price list.
What it doesn't do
It doesn't write replies, code or summaries. Where text is needed, we pair it with a language model and let Jev decide when that model is worth calling.

HOW WE IMPLEMENT IT

Enterprise rollout,
step by step.

Agree the labels
We design the categories with your team, with clear definitions and examples, and decide what a wrong answer costs for each one.
Build a test set
We label a sample of your real tickets or messages as the reference to measure against.
Measure before go-live
Jev runs in shadow mode next to your current process. We report precision and recall for every category, not one average.
Set confidence thresholds
High-confidence results are applied automatically; the rest go to a person. You choose the threshold per category.
Connect your tools
Webhooks from your helpdesk or app send the text; the result comes back as tags, fields or a routing decision.
Log and monitor
Every decision is recorded with its probabilities. We watch accuracy over time and update labels when your products or customers change.
Agree the data route
Direct API or through Cloudflare, data retention, and what text is sent. Personal data is minimised before it leaves your systems.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

What is Jev?
Jev is a classification model from TypeSafe AI. Instead of writing text, it answers structured questions about the text you send: yes or no with a probability, a choice from a list of options with a probability for each, or a score on a scale. One call can answer many questions at once.
What is Jev good for?
Decisions about text: which team a ticket belongs to, whether a message needs a person, which AI model should handle a request, whether content breaks a policy, or whether a field an AI extracted is likely wrong.
What can't Jev do?
It does not write replies, code or summaries. TypeSafe's own documentation says it is weak at counting and exact numbers, that accuracy drops when unrelated material is included, and that score levels should not be read as exact distances. We design around these limits.
How does Jev reduce AI costs?
It is priced per input token with no charge for output ($0.042 per million input tokens on TypeSafe's published price list). Used as a first step, it can send simple requests to a cheaper model and only call an expensive model when needed, or check an AI answer before you pay for a second pass.
How accurate is it on our tickets?
That depends on your categories and data, so we measure it before go-live: we label a sample of your past tickets, run Jev alongside your current process in shadow mode, and report accuracy per category. Low-confidence results always go to a person.
Does TypeSafe AI train on our data?
TypeSafe's privacy policy says it does not train or fine-tune models on customer inputs. Zero data retention is offered to enterprise customers, and Jev is also available through Cloudflare, which lists it with zero data retention. We agree the route with you before any data is sent.
Which tools can it connect to?
Any system that can send a webhook or call an API: helpdesks such as Zendesk, Freshdesk, Jira Service Management and ServiceNow, your own apps, and AI gateways such as Cloudflare AI Gateway, Vercel and LiteLLM.

No. Jev is a product of TypeSafe AI. We use it in client work and implement it for teams that want it.
