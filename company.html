Two operators. One standard.

Deepak and Xavier met during their MBA. After graduating, they went in opposite directions. Xavier joined an AI company, selling AI into enterprises. Deepak joined financial services, where he watched the institution he worked for hesitate on AI adoption. Deepak's engineering background let him sketch what the missing piece looked like, and Xavier saw the same gap from the seller's side.
