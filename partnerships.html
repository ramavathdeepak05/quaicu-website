TRACK · 01

Institutional Strategic Partnership

FOR: Vice-Chancellors · Deans · CIO Advisors · Chancellors · Hospital CIOs · Group CIOs.

A non-commercial pilot to put the product to work inside a real institution and prove what it actually returns, in hours saved and reports generated, before any commercial conversation begins.

WHAT PARTNERS DO
  • → Host a live pilot of the relevant product in the deployment they choose.
  • → Share what is and isn't working on the rules, the workflows, and the compliance handoffs.
  • → Review the pilot results alongside us and decide what graduates to full use.
WHAT PARTNERS RECEIVE
  • · First access to the product for their sector.
  • · A deployment they fully control.
  • · Direct influence on the rules, approvals, and audit features.
  • · A clear public story as a leader in responsible AI for their sector.
STRUCTURE
01
Pilot Partner.

Non-commercial. 90–120 days. Live deployment and validation.

02
Strategic Partner.

Post-pilot association. Case study and reference, optional.

TRACK · 02

Advisory & Consulting Partnership

FOR: Admissions consultants · placement partners · accreditation consultants · healthcare advisory firms · hospitality consultants · real estate advisors.
MODEL

The advisor keeps the institutional relationship and leads the strategic conversation. QUAICU builds, deploys, and runs the product. The relationship can be co-branded, white-labeled, or kept silent, whichever serves the client best.

WHAT PARTNERS RECEIVE
  • · Revenue participation on deployments.
  • · Clear account protection.
  • · Differentiation from generic advisory firms.
  • · Technical and delivery support from QUAICU.
